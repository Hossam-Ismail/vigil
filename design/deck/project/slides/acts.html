Each watch carries its own follow-up, set in the same sentence ("text me", "open a ticket in HubSpot") or picked in the app. iMessage runs through a small bridge built on Photon's Spectrum SDK on a Mac signed in to Messages; the text shown is exactly what Vigil sends. Vigil does not ask for feedback on every alert: it asks "Was this right?" only while a watch is new, when its double-check was unsure, or as an occasional spot check, and a YES or NO reply then labels that event and the watch retrains. HubSpot uses a private-app token and creates tickets, tasks or notes through the CRM API. Everything else goes through Slack or a webhook.
