Vigil is not a camera that narrates what it sees. You describe what matters to your business in one sentence, and Vigil turns that sentence into its own trained vision model, wires up the follow-up you asked for, and keeps improving it from every YES or NO you give it. The card shows one real kind of request: a truck arriving, text receiving, log it in HubSpot. In present mode the three things Vigil does with it build in one by one: it trains a model for exactly that request, it runs the follow-up (iMessage, HubSpot, Slack or a webhook), and it gets sharper every time someone answers.
