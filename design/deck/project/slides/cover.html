Vigil in one slide. It is a vision agent for the cameras a business already has: you describe what needs to be tracked, and Vigil trains a vision model for that exact request, does the follow-up when it happens, and learns from every answer. The request card is the kind of sentence people actually type: a truck arriving, text receiving, log it in HubSpot. The four pillars build in one by one. Vision: a small model trained for that one request on that camera, not a generic "person detected". Language: the whole setup is plain English, including how long something must last and what should happen next. Action: iMessage through Photon, HubSpot, Slack or any system through a webhook. Learning: every YES or NO becomes a training example and the watch retrains; on the Hard Hat Workers dataset the model went from 66.6% with words alone to 98.3% after learning, on images it never saw.
