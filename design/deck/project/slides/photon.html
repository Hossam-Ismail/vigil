Vigil · iMessage by Photon

04

Photon is the messaging layer that makes Vigil an agent people talk to instead of a dashboard they have to check. The loop: the per-watch model flags a frame, the larger model double-checks it, and Vigil sends the text and the photo through Photon to whoever the request named. The person answers YES or NO right in the thread; Photon delivers the reply back, Vigil matches it to the alert it answers, adds it to that watch's training set and retrains. Today the bridge is built on Photon's imessage-kit on a Mac signed in to Messages, so it works with any iPhone and nobody installs anything. The next slide shows a real message from that loop.
