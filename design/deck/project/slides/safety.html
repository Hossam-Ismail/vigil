Behind that text: every hard hat, checked

“Text the shift lead if anyone at the pump isn't wearing a hard hat.”

Vigil finds every person, then a model trained for this one question checks each head.

98.3%

of 2,673 heads called right, in CCTV and site photos it had never seen

Measured, not estimated. We trained Vigil's per-watch model on half of the Hard Hat Workers test set (real CCTV and construction photos) and scored it on the other half, 2,673 heads it had never seen: 98.3% correct when it had learned from all the labeled heads in the training half, 96.9% after 256, 94% after 32. That is the per-frame model alone, before the second-opinion check that runs on every alert. Heads smaller than 16 pixels were left out, as Vigil would not judge those either. The frame shown was not used for training; it is the same frame the shift lead's text on the previous slide carried.
