09

The whole setup is three things. A camera: any IP camera that speaks RTSP or ONVIF (most do), a USB webcam, or a phone in a browser tab. A computer on the same network: a Mac, or an Intel N150 mini PC from about $195; a Mac mini is what runs everything fully locally today. And the sentence, which says both what to watch for and what should happen next. iMessage alerts need a Mac signed in to Messages running the small Photon bridge; HubSpot needs a private-app token.
