Vigil

12

The whole setup is three things. A camera: any IP camera that speaks RTSP or ONVIF (most do), a USB webcam, or a phone in a browser tab. Any computer that can reach the camera's stream: the Mac or PC you already have on site, or a cloud server (cameras reach it over the network, through a VPN or forwarded RTSP stream, or a phone or webcam pushing from a browser tab). No special hardware to buy. On site, video never has to leave the building; in the cloud, it stays on your own server. And the sentence, which says both what to watch for and what should happen next. iMessage alerts need a Mac signed in to Messages running the small Photon bridge; HubSpot needs a private-app token.
