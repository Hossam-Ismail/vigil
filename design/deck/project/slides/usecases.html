Eight things people check by hand today, each written the way someone would say it, with the follow-up Vigil runs. Every one becomes its own small vision model on that customer's camera, and every one is something Vigil's watch types handle today: an object in a zone, a region's state held for a time, a count, or a line crossed in one direction. The Safety photo is real CCTV; the other seven are AI-generated example scenes (OpenAI gpt-image-2) used only to show the setting. Accuracy numbers in this deck come only from real footage.
